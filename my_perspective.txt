In my mind there are two dimensions to consider. Since we have background reader work and main thread reader work,
things get a bit hard to reason about. So considering latency is good. Understanding the impact of filtering decision
on latency is important. Equally we need to consider throughput when the database is saturated (either the main thread
or the background thread). This could be different because if the main thread becomes the bottleneck for example, then
the difference on the background may make no difference. This is useful to know. I believe we should consider these two
dimensions separately. For both, like I said before, we should vary corpus size and test a few different hybrid
queries:
- simple tag
- simple numeric
- simple text (single term match)
- complex text (AND/OR with fuzzy or something)
- complex mix (numeric, tag, text)
